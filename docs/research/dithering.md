# Ordered dithering as a background technique

Research note for the hero background on annamilazzo. Status: IN PROGRESS.

Question: can ordered (Bayer) dithering give the client the "chunky square pixel" look of
`@paper-design/shaders-react`'s `DitheringShader`, with the behaviour of a music-reactive
background, in the portfolio's palette, discreetly enough to sit under white hero copy on the
blue?

Verification note: every matrix in section 1 was computed twice by independent methods
(the Kronecker recurrence and the bit-interleave/bit-reverse identity) and the two agree.
See `scratchpad/bayer.js`.
