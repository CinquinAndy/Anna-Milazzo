# Client critique, round 2 — authoritative

Andy's review of the built site. This document outranks any research finding or design
proposal it contradicts. Where a research agent recommends restraint and this asks for
loudness, this wins.

## The verdict

Functionally sound. Visually a catastrophe: not playful, not colourful, not neo-brutalist
enough, and it does not feel like music. "Currently it's just another line in a CV."

## Per region

**Navbar.** Not creative at all. Wants something much closer to the Robin portfolio's nav
(a pill bar with icons, a star glyph, a filled active state) or Sound Wave's.

**Hero.** Catastrophic. Must be far more creative and must present *who she is and what she
does* far better than a name plus a tagline.

**Ground.** Too white, too bland. Wants a punchier pastel ground with more brand presence
and more impact — the Ubay reference's peach/cream, not our near-neutral paper.

**About and skills.** Must be genuinely creative, impactful, memorable. Currently reads as
a CV line.

**Folders.** A good start. Two problems: the tape does not sit well and must come in
**several colours**, and hovering a Folder shifts elements around — not smooth.

**The transport.** A catastrophe. You cannot tell it is a play control. Nobody understands
the little dashes next to the big bar. Visually illegible.

**Timeline.** The concept is right, the execution is not.

**Contact CTA.** Does not work. Wants a proper CTA block spanning 80-90% of the page width,
wide and confident, that makes you want to click.

## New reference: "Ubay"

An education marketing page. Peach/cream pastel ground — warm, clearly branded, not
neutral. Coral/salmon CTA buttons with hard shadows. Photo cards rotated a few degrees in
amber and sky blue, overlapping. **A black vinyl record sits behind the photo cluster** — a
music object used as pure composition. A circular badge sticker with text set on a curve.
A hand-drawn arrow doodle sweeping from the CTA toward the imagery. A full-width amber
stats bar carrying three huge numbers with a squiggle line drawn through it. Coral course
cards, each holding a photo, stacked in a row.

What to take: the warm pastel ground, the rotated overlapping photo cards, the vinyl as
composition, the badge sticker, the full-width stats bar, the confident coral CTA.
