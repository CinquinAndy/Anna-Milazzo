# Anna Milazzo, Portfolio

The public site of Anna Milazzo, a musician recently graduated from music school.
It exists to get her hired.

## Language

**Portfolio**:
The site itself. A body of work presented to be judged by someone deciding whether to
employ or commission Anna.
_Avoid_: landing page, site vitrine, showcase

**Recruiter**:
The primary reader. Someone assessing Anna's competence with a view to hiring her.
Every layout and copy decision answers to this reader first.
_Avoid_: user, visitor, customer

**Commission**:
Paid work where a client asks Anna to create sound to order. The secondary purpose of
the Portfolio, subordinate to being hired.
_Avoid_: prestation, gig, service, freelance work

**Song**:
One audio work by Anna, shown on the Portfolio with a title, a story, a cover image,
the audio itself, and an optional outbound link to a streaming platform. Covers a
composition, a study exercise and a Commission alike.
_Avoid_: Track, Piece, Project

**Folder**:
The card that presents one Song: a paper-folder shape with a tabbed top edge, the cover
image taped on like a CD sticker, and the title, story, player and link beside it.
_Avoid_: card, item, tile
