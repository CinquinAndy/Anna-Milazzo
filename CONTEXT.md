# Anna Milazzo, Portfolio

The public site of Anna Milazzo, a musician recently graduated from music school.
It exists to get her hired.

## Language

**Portfolio**:
The site itself. A body of work presented to be judged by someone deciding whether to
employ or commission Anna.
_Avoid_: landing page, site vitrine, showcase

**Recruiter**:
The primary reader. Someone assessing Anna's competence with a view to hiring her.
Every layout and copy decision answers to this reader first.
_Avoid_: user, visitor, customer

**Commission**:
Paid work where a client asks Anna to create sound to order. The secondary purpose of
the Portfolio, subordinate to being hired.
_Avoid_: prestation, gig, service, freelance work

**Song**:
One work by Anna, shown on the Portfolio with a title, a story, a cover image, its sound,
an optional outbound link to a streaming platform, and an optional video: a YouTube or
Vimeo link that becomes a player only when a visitor presses play (ADR-0013). The sound is
the audio, the video, or both: a Song whose video already carries its sound needs no audio
file (ADR-0006). Covers a composition, a study exercise, a short film's sound and a
Commission alike.
_Avoid_: Track, Piece, Project

**Folder**:
The one paper folder every Song is filed in, on the landing page: a row of Tabs along its
top edge, and inside it the Sheet of the Song whose Tab is open. Its body takes the open
Tab's colour (ADR-0014).
_Avoid_: card, carousel, slider, accordion, stack

**Tab**:
The numbered divider (01, 02…) on the Folder's top edge that opens one Song. Each has its
own colour, walked along the palette from blue to grape. One is open at a time.
_Avoid_: pill, page, onglet de navigation

**Sheet**:
The sheet of paper inside the Folder that carries one Song: a typed header with its number,
the cover taped on, and the title, story, player, video and link beside it. Where a Song
has no audio, its cover is the video's screen instead of a record sleeve.
_Avoid_: card, panel, slide
